Privacy Policy — RoadWorthy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

RoadWorthy ("the App") is operated by RoadWorthy Software ("we," "us," "our"). The App is a fleet safety and DOT compliance tool used by trucking companies to manage vehicle inspections, maintenance, driver credentials, and time tracking for their own employees and vehicles.

Who controls this data: RoadWorthy is provided to trucking companies ("Carriers") who create accounts for their own drivers and staff ("Users"). The Carrier is the data controller for the information described below — they decide what's entered and who has access to it within their own company account. RoadWorthy Software provides the app and the hosting infrastructure the Carrier's data lives on, but does not sell, share, or use Carrier or driver data for advertising, and has no independent business use for it beyond providing the service itself.

Information We Collect

Account and identity information

Name, email address, role (admin, driver, or mechanic), and company affiliation, entered by the Carrier when adding a person to their account. The email address is also used to send that person their account invitation and a link to set their password.

Driver credential information

CDL number, CDL class, license expiration date, license restrictions, CDL endorsements (Hazmat, Tanker, Doubles/Triples, Passenger, School Bus), DOT medical certificate expiration date, and TWIC (Transportation Worker Identification Credential) status and card number, where the Carrier chooses to record these. This information supports DOT/FMCSA regulatory compliance — it is information a trucking company is already required to track for each commercial driver. Within a company, it is visible only to the driver it belongs to, the company's admins, and staff the Carrier has given compliance access.

Location information

The App captures GPS location only at specific, discrete moments — never continuously and never in the background:

  • When a driver clocks in or out, to timestamp the record and confirm it happened at a location the Carrier has approved.
  • When an admin adds an approved work location (such as a yard), the admin's current position is recorded as that location's coordinates.

Location is not used to track a driver's movement or whereabouts at any other time.

Photos and video

Photos or short videos a driver chooses to attach to a vehicle issue report, to document a problem such as damage or a mechanical defect. These are stored privately and are visible only within the Carrier's company account.

Vehicle, inspection, and maintenance records

Pre-trip and post-trip DVIR (Driver Vehicle Inspection Report) checklist responses, defect reports, pass/fail results, a typed signature confirming the inspection, vehicle identifying information (unit number, VIN, make/model/year), tire and rim records, and maintenance history the Carrier logs.

Time and attendance records

Clock-in and clock-out times, any corrections made by a supervisor (with the reason recorded), and the hours and hours-of-service figures calculated from them.

Information stored on your device

So the App keeps working without a signal, inspections, issue reports, and clock punches made offline are saved on the phone until they can be sent, along with a recent copy of your own clock history. This data stays on the device and is cleared as it syncs or is replaced.

Technical information

Standard technical data generated by using a mobile app and connecting to our hosting provider (Supabase) — for example, IP address and device or error logs used for security and to keep the service running reliably. We do not use this data for advertising or tracking.

How We Use This Information

  • To provide the App's core functionality: DVIR inspections, defect tracking, maintenance records, time tracking, and driver credential management
  • To help Carriers meet DOT/FMCSA recordkeeping and Hours of Service obligations
  • To verify a Time Clock event happened at an approved location
  • To send account invitations and password-setup emails
  • To keep the App secure and working correctly

We do not sell this information, and we do not use it for advertising, either our own or anyone else's.

Who Can See This Information

Information is visible only within the Carrier's own company account — one trucking company cannot see another's data, and this is enforced by the database itself, not only by the App. Within a company account, what a driver can see versus what an admin can see is controlled by the Carrier through the App's roles and permissions. RoadWorthy Software staff may access data only as needed to provide technical support or maintain the service, never for any other purpose.

Service Providers

  • Supabase — hosted database, file storage, and sign-in, where App data is stored.
  • Hostinger — hosts this website and the email account that sends invitation and password-setup emails.

We rely on these providers' security and privacy commitments as our data processors. Data is encrypted in transit.

Data Retention

Information is retained for as long as the Carrier's account is active, and as needed to meet DOT/FMCSA recordkeeping requirements, which can require keeping certain inspection and driver records for a period of years.

Deleting Your Account or Data

To request deletion of your RoadWorthy account, email sales@roadworthysoftware.com from the address you use to sign in, with the subject "Account deletion request." A Carrier can also request deletion of their whole company's data the same way.

We will delete your sign-in account and personal details (name, email, and credential information). Records your employer is legally required to keep — such as submitted DVIR inspections and time records — may be retained for the period the law requires, after which they are deleted. Your employer can also ask us to deactivate your account, which immediately stops it from accessing any data.

Your Choices

Drivers: the information in your account is entered and controlled by your employer (the Carrier) as part of your employment. Questions about your own data should generally go to your employer first; you can also contact us directly using the information below.

Location, camera, and photo permissions can be turned off at any time in your device's settings, though this will stop the features that depend on them (Time Clock location checks and attaching photos or video to issue reports) from working as intended.

Children's Privacy

The App is a workplace tool for commercial drivers and fleet staff. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to sales@roadworthysoftware.com.
