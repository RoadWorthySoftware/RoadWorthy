# Privacy Policy — RoadWorthy

**Last updated: [FILL IN THE DATE YOU PUBLISH THIS]**

RoadWorthy ("the App") is operated by RoadWorthy Software ("we," "us," "our"). The App is a
fleet safety and DOT compliance tool used by trucking companies to manage vehicle inspections,
maintenance, driver credentials, and time tracking for their own employees and vehicles.

**Important context on who controls this data:** RoadWorthy is provided to trucking companies
("Carriers") who create accounts for their own drivers and staff ("Users"). The Carrier is the
data controller for the information described below — they decide what's entered and who has
access to it within their own company account. RoadWorthy Software provides the app and the
hosting infrastructure the Carrier's data lives on, but does not sell, share, or use Carrier or
Driver data for advertising, and does not have any independent business use for it beyond
providing the service itself.

## Information We Collect

### Account and identity information
Name, email address, role (admin/driver/mechanic), and company affiliation, entered by the
Carrier when creating a driver's account.

### Driver credential information
CDL number, CDL class, license expiration date, license restrictions, CDL endorsements (Hazmat,
Tanker, Doubles/Triples, Passenger, School Bus), DOT medical certificate expiration date, and
TWIC (Transportation Worker Identification Credential) status and card number, where the Carrier
chooses to record these. This information exists to support DOT/FMCSA regulatory compliance —
it is standard information a trucking company is already required to track for each commercial
driver.

### Location information
The App captures GPS location at specific, discrete moments — when a driver clocks in or out,
and when a driver starts a vehicle inspection — never continuously and never in the background.
This is used to timestamp records accurately and, for Time Clock specifically, to confirm a
clock-in happened at a location the Carrier has approved. Location is not used to track a
driver's movement or location at any other time.

### Photos and video
Photos or short videos a driver chooses to attach to a vehicle issue report, to document a
problem such as damage or a mechanical defect.

### Vehicle, inspection, and maintenance records
Pre-trip and post-trip DVIR (Driver Vehicle Inspection Report) checklist responses, defect
reports, pass/fail results, a typed signature confirming the inspection, vehicle identifying
information (unit number, VIN, make/model/year), and maintenance history the Carrier logs.

### Time and attendance records
Clock-in and clock-out timestamps and the hours calculated from them.

### Technical information
Standard technical data generated by using a mobile app and connecting to our hosting provider
(Supabase) — for example, IP address and device/error logs used for security and to keep the
service running reliably. We do not use this data for advertising or tracking.

## How We Use This Information

- To provide the App's core functionality: DVIR inspections, defect tracking, maintenance
  records, time tracking, and driver credential management
- To help Carriers meet DOT/FMCSA recordkeeping and Hours of Service obligations
- To verify a Time Clock event happened at an approved location
- To keep the App secure and working correctly

We do not sell this information, and we do not use it for advertising, either our own or anyone
else's.

## Who Can See This Information

Information is visible only within the Carrier's own company account — one trucking company
cannot see another's data. Within a company account, what a driver can see versus what an admin
can see is controlled by the Carrier through the App's role and permission settings. RoadWorthy
Software staff may access data only as needed to provide technical support or maintain the
service, never for any other purpose.

## Where Information Is Stored

Data is stored using Supabase, a hosted database and backend provider, with industry-standard
security practices including encryption in transit. We do not operate our own physical servers;
we rely on Supabase's infrastructure and their own security and privacy commitments as our data
processor.

## Data Retention

Information is retained for as long as the Carrier's account is active, and as needed to meet
DOT/FMCSA recordkeeping requirements (which can require retaining certain inspection and driver
records for a period of years). A Carrier can request deletion of their company's data by
contacting us at the email below, subject to any records we're legally required to retain.

## Your Choices

Drivers: the information in your account is entered and controlled by your employer (the
Carrier) as part of your employment. Questions about your own data should generally go to your
employer first; you can also contact us directly using the information below.

Location permission can be turned off at any time in your device's settings, though this will
prevent Time Clock and inspection features that depend on it from working as intended.

## Children's Privacy

The App is a workplace tool for commercial drivers, who must be adults holding a valid CDL. It
is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

after a change means you accept the updated policy.
